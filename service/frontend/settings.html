Component Training

Words are broken into hanzi components (radicals and sub-parts) for the character breakdown and component quiz. Instead of training every component, pick a target: as new words start training, only enough components — starting with whichever unlocks the most words you haven't covered yet — are added to reach that share of your Chinese vocabulary. Already-trained components are never removed by this setting.

%

Learn as many components as needed to cover this share of all your Chinese words. Set to 0 to train every component (default).

API Keys

Your keys are encrypted using a key derived from your login password (PBKDF2-SHA256) and stored in the database. They are only accessible while you are logged in. If you change your password, your keys are automatically re-encrypted. If you forget your password, you will need to re-enter your API keys.

Personal API keys let you use DeepL translation and LLM scene generation without needing a plus account or a personal LLM key.
