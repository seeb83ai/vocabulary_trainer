Mnemonics

Your Hanzi Movie Method library. Each character becomes a movie scene: an actor (pinyin initial) in a location (pinyin final), in a room of that location (tone), with props (components).

Pick a person for each pinyin initial. People whose names start with that sound are easiest to recall.

Pick a place you know well for each final.

Each tone is a room or area within the location. The defaults follow the Mandarin Blueprint method.

Each component maps to an object. Defaults are provided; override them or add your own.
