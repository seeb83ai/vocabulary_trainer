This app uses free data, fonts and code from other projects. Thank you to their authors. Each entry shows the source and its license.

Dictionaries and word lists

  • CC-CEDICT · CC BY-SA 4.0

    English translations and dictionary lookup.

  • HanDeDict · CC BY-SA 2.0 DE

    German translations and dictionary lookup.

  • Make Me a Hanzi · LGPL-3.0

    Character breakdown: radicals, components and etymology.

  • complete-hsk-vocabulary · MIT

    HSK 2.0 and HSK 3.0 word lists.

  • FrequencyWords · CC BY-SA 4.0

    Word frequency: the order of new words and the ranking of translations. The lists come from OpenSubtitles. We filtered the Chinese, English and German lists and use the first 8,000 entries of each. Our changed lists also have the CC BY-SA 4.0 license.

  • mp3-chinese-pinyin-sound · Public domain (Unlicense)

    Recordings of the pinyin syllables.

  • studycli.org Chinese grammar guide · © studycli.org

    Example sentences of the grammar list.
